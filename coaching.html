Common Questions

Things people
usually ask first.

Straight answers. If something isn't covered here, book a discovery call — that's what they're for.

Book a Free Call
Can I book the 12-week programme directly?
No. Start with a free 20-minute Discovery Call with Brenda or Caroline. You talk through what you need, check the programme is the right fit and ask any questions. If you decide to go ahead, your coach will agree payment with you.
Do I have to be going through perimenopause?
Not at all. Nutricore Health works with women 35 and above — whether perimenopause is relevant to you or not. The five-pillar approach applies to anyone who wants to feel better, has more energy, sleeps properly, and has a plan that fits real life. Perimenopause is one context, not a requirement.
How do I choose between Brenda and Caroline?
Brenda and Caroline both deliver the same 12-week programme. Your named coach manages your coaching and is your main point of contact, and the wider Nutricore Health team is available behind the scenes when extra expertise would help. Brenda is also a qualified chef and runs the Food Inspiration Session. Book a free Discovery Call with whichever of them you would like to speak to first.
I've tried things like this before and they haven't worked. Why would this be different?
Fair question. The honest answer is that most programmes fail because they're built around what worked for someone else, not you. The Nutricore approach starts with your health intake — a proper look at your history, your habits, and your life — before any recommendations are made. There's no fixed script. Your coach follows your priorities, not a syllabus. If you've done generic before and it didn't stick, that's a reasonable reason to try something built around you.
How much time will this take each week?
One 1:1 session every two weeks, plus a short weekly check-in that takes a few minutes to complete. The work itself, the habit changes and the small shifts, is built around your actual schedule. This is not a programme that adds to your to-do list. It's designed to work with the life you already have.
Will I be told what to eat or given a meal plan?
No rigid meal plans. Nutricore Health is food-first, not restrictive. You'll get practical, evidence-informed nutrition guidance — recipe suggestions, food strategies, and support around the way you actually eat. Nothing is off the table. The goal is a relationship with food that works for your hormones, your gut, and your life. Not a diet with an end date.
Are supplements part of the programme?
Supplements are not a core part of the programme. Your plan starts with sleep, resilience, nutrition, movement and joy. If supplements come up, your coach will talk it through with you within the scope of their role.
What happens after the 12 weeks?
The programme ends with a final review of your progress and personalised next steps. What happens after that is for you and your coach to talk through. There's no pressure either way.
