The Business Case

The data is clear.
Women's health costs your business.

Untreated health challenges don't stay personal. They show up as absence, reduced output, and lost talent. The employers taking this seriously are seeing the returns.

9.4 days

Average sickness absence per UK employee in 2025 — a record high, up from 7.8 days in 2023. Mental ill-health is now the leading cause of long-term absence.

CIPD Health & Wellbeing at Work Report, 2025
25%

Women are 25% more likely than men to report that their job negatively impacts their mental health. In 2024/25 there were 3,220 cases of work-related stress, depression or anxiety per 100,000 female workers.

Health & Safety Executive Annual Statistics, Dec 2025
40%+

Of women experiencing severe hormonal symptoms — menstruation, menopause, or fertility-related — report working through the pain without taking time off or receiving support.

Deloitte Women @ Work Global Outlook, 2024
15.5%

Of productive time lost by female employees to presenteeism — higher than male counterparts at 13.3%. Physical inactivity, chronic conditions, and mental health challenges all contribute.

RAND Europe / Britain's Healthiest Workplace Report
54%

Of employers who invest in targeted wellbeing report improved overall health outcomes. 39% see increased employee engagement, and 38% see measurable gains in workplace performance.

CIPD Health & Wellbeing at Work Report, 2025

Five pillars.
One measurable impact
on your people.

Each pillar maps directly to a business outcome. This isn't wellness for wellness' sake.

01 Sleep Cognitive performance

42% of UK employees report poor sleep quality. Sleep deprivation directly compromises decision-making, focus, and emotional regulation at work.

02 Resilience Reduced burnout & absence

Women aged 35–44 carry disproportionate workplace stress. Building nervous system capacity reduces burnout risk and long-term sickness.

03 Nutrition Sustained energy & focus

Food that supports hormones and gut health translates directly to consistent energy, better mood, and fewer sick days.

04 Movement Output & physical health

Over 40% of women experiencing hormonal symptoms work through pain without support. The right movement strategy changes that.

05 Joy Retention & engagement

Women who lose joy at work leave. Coaching that addresses quality of life and workplace satisfaction directly supports retention.
